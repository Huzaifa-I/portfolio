01New productPrototype + API

BIN Intelligence: know a card before you charge it

The first digits of every card number (the BIN) reveal what kind of card it is and which bank issued it. I designed a product that turns those digits into useful answers for online shops.

The problem

Shops only learn what a card is after they charge it. The data they buy is often out of date, and nobody tells them how a bank is likely to behave.

What I did

I wrote the strategy, the pricing and the requirements, and built a clickable console plus a working API, so people could try the product for themselves.

The result

A clear plan in three stages, and one simple privacy rule that also sets the product apart: a shop only ever sees its own numbers.

02Internal toolAI

A planning assistant that turns weeks into an hour

Every new payment provider needs someone to read its documents, compare it with what we already have, and write a plan for engineers. That reading was slowing everything down.

The problem

Each plan took one to two weeks, and quality depended on who wrote it.

What I did

I designed a six step AI assisted process, with a model plan that sets the standard and a person who reviews every result.

The result

Plans now take under an hour, and more than 30 have been written this way.

  1. Check accessMake sure we can log in to the provider's test system
    Automatic
  2. Read the documentsPull out what the provider offers and how it works
    Automatic
  3. CompareMatch it with the payment flows we already support
    Automatic
  4. Find who is affectedList the teams and systems the work will touch
    Automatic
  5. Write the planProduce the requirements on the standard template
    Human review
  6. Create the tasksOpen the engineering tickets in Jira
    Automatic

04RoadmapSimulator

Making card security easy to see, then proving it

3-D Secure is the extra check your bank sometimes asks for when you pay online. It stops fraud, but too many checks make people give up. There were 23 ideas to improve it, and no shared picture.

What I did

I built a simulator that plays each idea step by step, then small testers that send real test payments to providers to check each assumption.

The result

Long debates got shorter because everyone could see the difference. Three provider testers turned guesses into proof.
